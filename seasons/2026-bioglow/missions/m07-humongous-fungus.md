# M07 — Humongous Fungus

**Model(s):** mycelium (extends), plant root
**Location:** shared area with M06 — see [field-map.md](../field-map.md)

## Objective
Fully extend the mycelium network toward a neighboring tree root.

## Scoring
- The mycelium is completely extended — **20**
- Bonus: and a connection is formed between one team's extended mycelium and the **opposing team's** fully extended plant root — **+10 added** (two bonuses possible — one per team)

## Constraints & gotchas
- The mycelium and plant root **do not need to touch** — both just need to be fully extended.
- **Bonus is impossible in remote competition or with no opposing team.** Don't build strategy around it for remote events.
- If both teams fully extend their pieces, both get the bonus — cooperative, not competitive.
