# M03 — Flip the Rock

**Model(s):** rock, research flag
**Location:** see [field-map.md](../field-map.md)

## Objective
Flip the rock to lower the research flag, then return the rock to exactly where it started ("leave no trace").

## Scoring
- The research flag is down — **20**
- Bonus: and the rock has been returned to its original starting position — **+10**

## Constraints & gotchas
- Bonus needs the rock **back at its start position**, so a plain flip-and-leave only gets 20. Need a mechanism that flips then re-seats it, or flips it a full 360°.

## Setup / starting state
Rock in its start position, flag up.
