# M04 — Lucky Leaves

**Model(s):** nest (holds the leaves + katydid), 2 leaves, katydid
**Location:** see [field-map.md](../field-map.md)

## Objective
Collect the leaves off the nest without disturbing the camouflaged katydid.

## Scoring
- One leaf completely removed, no longer touching the nest — **10**
- Bonus: and the second leaf completely removed, no longer touching the nest, **with the katydid in its original starting position** — **+20**

## Constraints & gotchas
- **If the katydid is completely removed from the nest at any time → bonus is gone for good.**
- **If the katydid ends up outside the leaf habitat (even partly) → 0 points for the whole mission.** The leaf habitat = the nest + the marked area on the mat around it.
- So the safe play is a gentle leaf grab that never touches the katydid.

## Setup / starting state
**Randomized by the referee at match start** — katydid and leaf positions are not fixed. The robot approach must tolerate variation, or use a sensor.
