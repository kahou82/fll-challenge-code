# M13 — Keystone Species

**Model(s):** restoration platform, young trees. Plus a **team-built keystone species model** (from bag 20).
**Location:** interchangeable dock (mine / city / farm) — see [field-map.md](../field-map.md)

## Objective
Build your own "keystone species" model and deliver it onto the restoration platform, with the young trees raised.

## Scoring
- Your team's keystone species is on the restoration platform, **and** the young trees are raised — **30**

## Constraints & gotchas
- **The keystone species model counts as equipment** — it goes through pre-match inspection and counts toward the one-launch-area 20 pt bonus. Referees will ask you to identify it.
- Build it from **bag 20** bricks.
- Both parts of the condition needed: model on platform **and** trees raised.
- This is a dock model — decide which dock (mine/city/farm) in [strategy.md](../strategy.md). Dock choice interacts with M14 and M15.

## Setup / starting state
Young trees down; restoration platform empty; keystone model starts in Home as equipment.
