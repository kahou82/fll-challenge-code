# M01 — Drone Survey

**Model(s):** drone, stalk, LiDAR map, scan marker
**Location:** far side of the field — see [field-map.md](../field-map.md)

## Objective
Launch the drone off its stalk so it's no longer on the mat, and flip the LiDAR map over so the scan marker lands in the survey area.

## Scoring
- Drone is no longer touching the mat — **20**
- Bonus: and the LiDAR map is completely flipped over, with the scan marker at least partly in the survey area — **+10**

## Constraints & gotchas
- "Completely flipped over" — a partial flip doesn't count.
- Scan marker only needs to be *partly* in the survey area.

## Setup / starting state
Drone resting on/against the stalk; LiDAR map unflipped.
