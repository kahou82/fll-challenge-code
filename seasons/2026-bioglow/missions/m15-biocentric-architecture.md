# M15 — Biocentric Architecture

**Model(s):** building with nesting canopy, garden skylight, compost hatch
**Location:** interchangeable dock (mine / city / farm) — see [field-map.md](../field-map.md)

## Objective
Modify the building for wildlife and soil health: raise the nesting canopy, push the garden skylight in, open the compost hatch.

## Scoring
- The nesting canopy is raised — **10**
- The garden skylight is completely in — **10**
- The compost hatch is completely opened, touching the mat — **10**
- **Environmental Bonus** — if the model is on the dock matching its greatest ecological need — **+10 added** (one only):
  - **mine dock** → nesting canopy
  - **city dock** → garden skylight
  - **farm dock** → compost hatch

## Constraints & gotchas
- Three independent 10s + one conditional 10.
- The bonus is a **dock placement decision made before the match** — see [strategy.md](../strategy.md). Putting M15 on the "wrong" dock forfeits the +10.
- Only one environmental bonus possible even though there are three sub-features.

## Setup / starting state
Canopy down, skylight out, compost hatch closed. Model on whichever dock we chose.
